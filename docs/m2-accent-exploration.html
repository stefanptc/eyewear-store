Seven warm candidates. Same type, same product.

Brief. The accent is the one place colour gets to speak in a monochrome system. It lives on the price, italic emphasis in product names, link underlines, the active state in the lens calculator, and selected pills. Each stripe below renders the same product mock with one accent applied in five places, so you read the colour in context — not as an abstract swatch. Sorted by intensity: bold-warm first, mid-warm middle, quiet-warm last.

How to read these

Read top to bottom in order. The stripes are arranged by intensity — bold accents shout, quiet accents whisper. Notice which one stops you. That's usually the right answer.

Focus on the "Mărășești" italic. The product name's italic emphasis is the accent's biggest spotlight. If it reads cleanly there, it'll read everywhere. If it fights with the body text or feels off, the rest doesn't matter.

My read, briefly: for the Cubitts-direction we locked, 02 Antique Brass is the most direct match. 06 Mushroom Taupe is the most disciplined — almost no colour at all, which is its own kind of premium signal. 05 Terracotta is the most "Romanian-specific," which is either a feature or a constraint depending on whether you want geographic identity in the brand. 03 Bottle Green is the dark horse — it has actual eyewear-shop heritage (apothecary green is on half the old optical signs in Europe) and would be the most ownable.
